My teaching covers ecology, biodiversity conservation and agroecosystems at Bachelor's and Master's levels. At the University of Hohenheim and Ulm University, I have contributed to lectures, seminars, practical classes and field courses. I connect ecological concepts with examples from research and support students in developing skills in study design, field methods, data analysis and scientific communication.

Supervision and mentoring

I have provided primary, day-to-day supervision for 16 completed theses (six Bachelor's and ten Master's), alongside co-supervision of additional Master's projects. I have also mentored five doctoral researchers in study design, data management and statistical analysis.

I work with students from diverse cultural and academic backgrounds and adapt my teaching and supervision to their experience, quantitative preparation and language confidence. I use clear expectations, transparent assessment criteria and individual feedback, and discuss adjustments when personal circumstances affect participation or progress. In field courses and research teams, I emphasise respectful collaboration, peer support and shared responsibility for safety.
