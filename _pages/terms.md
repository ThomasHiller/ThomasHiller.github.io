## Privacy notice

This notice explains the processing associated with visiting this website and contacting its operator. The person responsible for processing within the operator's control is Dr. Thomas Hiller, whose contact details appear above.

### Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Delivering its pages requires your browser to connect to GitHub's infrastructure. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes.

For processing within my control that is necessary to provide and secure this website, the proposed legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The relevant interests are making my academic work accessible and maintaining a reliable, secure website.

GitHub determines retention for its own service logs. Its general privacy statement describes retention according to the processing purpose and applicable obligations; it does not specify a single retention period for GitHub Pages visitor logs. GitHub processes data internationally, including in the United States, and describes its transfer safeguards in its privacy statement.

Further information: [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Website statistics: GoatCounter

I use GoatCounter to understand the readership of this website and its pages. The enabled statistics are page and visitor counts, referring websites and campaign information, browser and operating system, screen size and country. Region collection is enabled for the United States, Russia and China. Language collection and storage of individual pageview records are disabled.

Statistics are stored in aggregate form. Session counting distinguishes repeat visits for up to eight hours. GoatCounter states that this temporarily processes the site identifier, IP address and browser header in memory; IP addresses and full browser headers are not stored in its analytics database. Visitor analytics do not use cookies or browser storage.

The data-retention setting is **0 days**, meaning automatic deletion is disabled. Aggregate statistics remain until deleted. This is separate from the eight-hour session period. The hosted service is operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland, with data stored at Hetzner in Germany and Finland. After account deletion, some backup data may remain for up to 30 days.

Further information: [GoatCounter Privacy Policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

### Browser preferences

The website saves your selected light or dark colour theme in your browser's local storage under the key `theme`. This preference remains until it is changed or the site's browser data is cleared. It is used to remember your selected appearance.

### Externally loaded resources

The website loads Academicons and MathJax resources from jsDelivr. These provide academic icons and support the display of mathematical notation. Loading them causes your browser to connect to the external provider, transmitting technical information including your IP address and the requested resource.

Further information: [jsDelivr Privacy Policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy).
